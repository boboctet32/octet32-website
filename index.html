// 01 — The Problem

Network complexity
has outpaced the tools

Enterprises and blockchain organizations today rely on outdated tools — spreadsheets, simple databases, or legacy IPAM systems — that fail to keep pace with modern network growth and the explosive rise of IoT.

⚠ Downtime

Network Outages & Conflicts

IP address conflicts and misconfigurations bring businesses to a halt. A single misconfigured network can cost hundreds of thousands in lost revenue per hour.

⚠ M&A Risk

M&A Integration Chaos

When companies merge, network address spaces collide — creating immediate system-wide disruption that demands massive engineering hours or risks critical failures.

⚠ IoT Scale

Explosive Device Growth

The proliferation of IoT devices is an incoming tsunami. Current manual tools are not scalable for smart city, industrial, and enterprise deployments of tomorrow.

⚠ Visibility

Lost Address Space

Without a centralized system, free address space becomes invisible to engineers — creating artificial scarcity while real capacity goes unrecognized and wasted.
